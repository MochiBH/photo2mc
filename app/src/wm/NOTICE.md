# Watermelon UI components

The files in this folder are adapted from Watermelon UI (https://ui.watermelon.sh,
source: github.com/WatermelonCorp/watermelon-platform), MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2026 Watermelon Platform Contributors.

They were converted from TSX to JSX and made controlled/parameterised for this app:

- `button.jsx`, `card.jsx`, `badge.jsx`, `input.jsx` from `src/components/ui/`
- `fluid-tabs.jsx` from `animated-components/fluid-tabs` (label shows on the active tab only, so six fit on a phone)
- `continuous-tabs.jsx` from `animated-components/continuous-tabs` (controlled, scrollable, per-instance pill)
- `shimmer-button.jsx` from `animated-components/shimmer-button`
- `tick-progress.jsx` from the header of `animated-components/onboarding-checklist`
- `shimmer-progress.jsx` from the bar in `animated-components/labeled-progress-indicator`
- `expand.jsx` from `animated-components/expand-details`
